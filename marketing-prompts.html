Best AI Marketing Prompts (2026)

Free, copy-paste prompts that produce real marketing output. Tested on ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini.

1. Cold Email That Gets Replies

Write a cold outreach email for [product/service] targeting [job title] at [company type]. Constraints: - Subject line: max 6 words, curiosity-driven - Body: max 5 sentences total - CTA: a question, not a pitch - Tone: peer-to-peer, not salesy Write 3 variations: results-focused, curiosity-focused, pain-point-focused.

2. Landing Page Copy Formula

Write landing page copy for [product] targeting [audience]. Structure: 1. Hero: Headline (max 10 words) + subheadline 2. Problem: 3 pain points (use "you" language) 3. Solution: Features → Benefits 4. Social proof: 3 testimonial quotes 5. CTA: Button text + supporting text Do NOT use: "revolutionize", "game-changing", "leverage", "synergy"

3. Facebook/Instagram Ad Copy

Create 5 ad variations for [product] targeting [audience]. Each ad needs: - Hook (first line, max 10 words) - Body (2-3 sentences, problem→solution) - CTA Styles: 1) Pain→solution 2) Curiosity 3) Social proof 4) Contrarian 5) Urgency

4. Email Sequence Builder

Create a 5-email welcome sequence for [product/service] targeting [audience]. For each email: - Subject line (max 8 words) - Preview text (max 40 chars) - Body (under 200 words) - Single CTA Sequence flow: Welcome → Value → Story → Objection handling → Offer Spacing: emails 1-2 daily, then every 2 days

5. SEO Blog Brief

Create an SEO content brief for the keyword: [target keyword] Include: - Title tag (under 60 chars, keyword front-loaded) - Meta description (under 155 chars, include CTA) - H2/H3 outline with key points per section - 5 related keywords to include naturally - 3 internal linking opportunities - Suggested word count based on top-ranking competition - Content angle that's different from page 1 results

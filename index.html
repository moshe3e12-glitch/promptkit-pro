MARKETING#47

The Cold Email That Gets Replies

Write a cold outreach email for [product/service] targeting [job title] at [company type].


Constraints:

- Subject line: max 6 words, curiosity-driven, no clickbait

- Opening line: reference something specific about their company

- Body: max 5 sentences total

- Must include one specific, quantified result

- CTA: a question, not a pitch

- Tone: peer-to-peer, not salesy


Write 3 variations: results-focused, curiosity-focused, and pain-point-focused.

💡 Pro tip: Fill in the brackets with real details — the more specific your inputs, the more personalized and effective the output.
